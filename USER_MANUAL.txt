Welcome to Aakaash! This manual will guide you through all the features of 
Bangladesh's smartest weather and natural calamity app.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GETTING STARTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Setting Your Location:
  When you open Aakaash, it defaults to Dhaka. Tap the "Search" icon (magnifying 
  glass) at the top of the Home screen to search for your district or upazila. 
  You can also use the GPS button to detect your current location.

* App Theme:
  Tap the "Theme" icon (sun/moon) to switch between Light Mode, Dark Mode, or 
  System Default. The app also features adaptive gradients that change based on 
  the current weather conditions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WEATHER FORECAST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Home screen is divided into 4 main segments:
* Today: Shows the current temperature, feels like, weather condition, and a 
  grid of key metrics (humidity, wind speed, UV index, pressure, visibility, 
  dew point).
* Hourly: A 24-hour strip showing temperature and rain probability every 3 hours.
* 5 Days: A day-by-day breakdown for the next 5 days, including min/max 
  temperatures and precipitation chances.
* Details: Additional in-depth weather information.
* AI Weather Insights: (Premium) Get natural-language weather summaries and 
  smart, context-aware suggestions (like "Carry an umbrella") tailored just for you.
* Home Screen Widget: Long-press your Android launcher to add the Aakaash widget, 
  which keeps your selected city and real-time temperature right on your home screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. AI WEATHER INSIGHTS (Premium)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Aakaash uses Google Gemini to read your local forecast and explain it to you 
in natural, conversational language.
* Context-Aware Summaries: Get a 2-3 sentence breakdown of temperature shifts, 
  rain probability, and practical advice tailored for Bangladesh.
* Smart Suggestions: The AI dynamically generates action chips (like "Carry an 
  umbrella" or "Stay hydrated") based on extreme weather triggers.
* Accessing: The AI Briefing card is automatically displayed at the top of the 
  Home screen for premium subscribers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FARMER'S CORNER (কৃষকের কোণ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Aakaash provides smart agricultural advice tailored to Bangladesh's climate.
* Accessing: Tap the "More" icon (⋮) at the top right of the Home screen and 
  select "Farmer's Corner".
* Farming Conditions Score: A 0-100 gauge showing overall agricultural 
  suitability for today.
* Advisories: You will find 7 color-coded expandable cards with advice on:
  - Pesticide Spraying 
  - Seed Sowing
  - Irrigation
  - Harvest Readiness
  - Extreme Weather (Heatwaves, Cold waves, Nor'westers)
  - Pest & Disease Risk
  - Fertilizer Application
* Seasonal Info: Shows the current Bengali season (e.g., Grisma, Barsha) and 
  key farming activities for the season.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. NATURAL CALAMITIES & EARTHQUAKES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Aakaash monitors Bangladesh for real-time disasters.
* Accessing the Radar: Tap the "More" icon (⋮) and select "Natural Calamities".
* The Radar Map: Displays active earthquakes, floods, cyclones, and fires on a 
  map of Bangladesh.
* Flood Fusion: Aakaash combines river discharge data with rainfall forecasts 
  to provide district-level flood risks.
* Earthquake Sentinel: Aakaash checks for earthquakes every 15 minutes. If a 
  significant earthquake (Magnitude 3.5+) occurs in or near Bangladesh, you will 
  receive an instant, loud push notification.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tap the "Bell" icon at the top of the Home screen to manage notifications.
* Daily Weather: Get a morning summary of today's forecast. You can change the 
  time (default is 7:00 AM).
* Calamity Alerts: Background checks every 6 hours for floods/storms near you. 
  You can adjust the alert radius (in km).
* Earthquake Alerts: Instant alerts for regional earthquakes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. SUBSCRIPTION (BDApps)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Aakaash gives you 3 free location searches per day. To unlock unlimited 
searches and active background notifications, you need to subscribe.
* How to Subscribe: Tap the "Subscribe" button on the Home screen.
* Payment: Enter your mobile number and verify the OTP. You will be charged 
  BDT 2.00/day by your mobile operator (Robi, Cirkle).
  No credit card is required.
* Unsubscribing: Tap the "Subscribe" chip again on the Home screen to manage 
  or cancel your subscription at any time.
